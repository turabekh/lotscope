# PA Right-to-Know Law request — City of Harrisburg code-enforcement records

*Draft for the owner to file (Track-C handoff). Submit via the City's online form (`laserfiche.harrisburgpa.gov/Forms/Righ-to-Know-Request`) or by email/mail to the Open Records Officer, Office of the City Solicitor, Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. City Government Center, 10 North Second Street, Suite 402, Harrisburg, PA 17101. The City has 5 business days to respond under 65 P.S. § 67.901.*

---

To: Open Records Officer, City of Harrisburg (Office of the City Solicitor)

Re: **Right-to-Know Law request for code-enforcement / property-maintenance violation records**

Pursuant to the Pennsylvania Right-to-Know Law, 65 P.S. § 67.101 et seq., I request access to and an electronic copy of the following public records held by the City of Harrisburg's codes administration / bureau of codes enforcement:

**Records requested:** All code-enforcement and property-maintenance violation records for properties within the City of Harrisburg for the period **January 1, 2020 to the present**, including for each violation the following fields where maintained:
1. Property street address;
2. County parcel identifier / tax parcel number, where associated with the record;
3. Violation type and/or the code section cited;
4. Current status (e.g., open, complied, referred, abated);
5. Date the violation was opened/issued and date closed/resolved.

**Format:** Please provide the records in **electronic tabular form (CSV or Microsoft Excel)** — an export from the codes-enforcement system of record — rather than scanned images, so the data can be processed programmatically. If an export template already exists, that format is acceptable.

**Recurring production:** To the extent the Law and City practice permit, I would welcome arranging to receive the same dataset on a **recurring quarterly basis**; please advise whether a standing request or a data-sharing arrangement is available.

I am willing to pay reasonable duplication fees permitted under the Law and the Office of Open Records fee schedule; **please notify me of any fees exceeding $50 before processing.** If any portion of this request is denied, please cite the specific exemption under 65 P.S. § 67.708 (or other legal basis) for each withheld field, and provide the remainder.

Please direct the response and any questions to: **[your name, email, phone, mailing address]**.

Thank you for your assistance.

*[Signature / date]*

---

## Notes for the owner
- This is a standard records request; there is no fee to file, only possible duplication costs (the $50 notice cap above protects you).
- If the City has no such electronic system (some smaller municipalities track violations on paper), the response will say so — which itself confirms the "no data source" state is correct, and we keep that classification honestly.
- When the first delivery arrives, drop the file into `backend/corpus/hbg-violations/` and tell me its columns — the loader is already built and just needs the field mapping.
