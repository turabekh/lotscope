## The bug (caught by verification, not assumption)
A vacant Harrisburg flood parcel (`hbg:01-032-022`) intersects a `flood_zone` polygon (`100 Year`), yet
its stored verdict was `risk_score 0`, no flood flag — even after a full hbg verdict recompute. Trace:
`risk/scoring.py::_classify_flood_zone` recognized SFHA only via `_SFHA_ZONE_PREFIXES = ("A","V")` plus
`0.2`/`SHADED` markers. The city `Flood_Parcels` layer labels zones **`100 Year`** (1%-annual = SFHA) and
**`500 Year`** (0.2% = moderate) → matched nothing → classified `minimal` → no flag, risk 0. **So the
entire flood-loading effort produced zero verdict impact until this fix.**
