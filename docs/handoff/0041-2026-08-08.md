## 5. Operational facts

- **Derivation:** `make derive-phillycode ARGS="--title N --max-batch-chars 12000 --pace-seconds 22"`. Idempotent per title; a failed title costs only itself since #133. Under the free tier expect roughly one title per 2–15 minutes and occasional `ReadTimeout` — re-run and it resumes.
- **Do not remove the per-source partial HNSW indexes** (`f3a92c1d5e78`). Retrieval filters by `source` over one graph; without them 21k phillycode rows would silently gut title14 recall. The regression check is a before/after top-5 probe on three fixed queries.
- **Title 9 / §9-3010 is settled** — Bill 210740-A deleted former §9-3007 and renumbered; the TOC was never regenerated. Do not re-investigate.
- **Table continuations are fixed** in the parser via column-grid remapping, enforced by gate 5. Do not re-litigate the two rejected variants.
- CI has `paths-ignore: docs/**, **/*.md`, so a docs-only branch reporting "no checks" is correct, not broken.
- `make fmt-check` is a separate gate from `make lint`; prettier failures do not surface in `pnpm lint`.
- Prettier reflows Vue `computed` blocks, so string-matching edits against them need a re-read first.
