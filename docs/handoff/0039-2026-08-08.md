### 2.1 Valuation confidence (#119)

The land value appears on five surfaces. `VerdictPanel` and `WhyScoreDrawer` already showed a confidence figure and `comp-tier.ts` already named a wider search — but the **hero** asserted *"Estimated from recent recorded comparable sales nearby"* directly above a panel reading *"No land sales were found close by"*, and the **PDF** carried a bare range. 381,155 of 460,481 priced verdicts (82.8%) are low confidence, so the unqualified wording was the common case.

The band is inverted from the same `CONFIDENCE_SCORE` dict `composition.py` already used, so score and band cannot drift.

### 2.2 Harrisburg districts in Philadelphia (#120)

`phl` held 48 standards; Philadelphia has 39. The other nine — `CG, CN, DC, IND, INS, OSR, RF, RL, RM` — were Harrisburg's, stamped `hbg-provisional-2026`.

**Both seeders were already correct.** This was residue from a bug fixed earlier that the seed could never clear: it upserts on `(market_code, code, code_version)` and **never deletes**, so a stale row written once survives every run. Hence a migration. No parcel resolved to any of the nine, but `RM` was a live trap — Philadelphia has RM-1…RM-4, and a parcel reading exactly `RM` would have received Harrisburg's unreviewed provisional values.

A guard test asserts the invariant that found it: **a `code_version` belongs to exactly one market.**

### 2.3 What the Quick Guide actually says (#121)

The owner supplied `quick-guide-2026-02.pdf`, so these come from the primary source.

- **RMX-1/2/3 require an approved master plan.** Table 14-701-2 states it plainly; our seed said `requires_master_plan = false`. **517 parcels** were being told they could build by right in a district permitting neither by-right nor variance.
- **CMX-3/4/5 have no density row**, so `max_units` and `lot_area_per_unit_sqft` are `unregulated`, not uncaptured.
- **The master-plan screening branch was unreachable.** `decide()` ran `_unavailable_critical` *before* it, and `by_right_units` is deliberately `None` for a master-plan district — so every such parcel reported `insufficient_data`, *"Core buildability could not be established"*. Buildability **was** established. Measured before the fix: **518 of 518** said `insufficient_data`; zero said `high_entitlement_risk`. This predated the RMX change and made handoff 0037's claim about master-plan screening untrue in practice.

### 2.4 Parking (#122, ADR-0077)

`min_parking_spaces` was the wrong shape and is **dropped, not filled**: Chapter 14-800 is a use-category × district-group matrix whose cells are often conditional formulas. Restricted to household living — the only thing modelled on a vacant lot — the tables are plain ratios.

Measured on production, 250 spread-sampled parcels through the real service: **97.6% of rows now answer**, **79.6% answer that no off-street parking is required at all.**

Three deviations from the ADR as proposed are recorded in it: a module constant rather than a seeded table; `pass` rather than `not_applicable` for the zero case, because a requirement of zero **is** a stated standard; and layout-preserving extraction in place of visual verification.

**That last one changed the outcome.** Naive extraction of Table 14-802-3 returned five column headers against three values per row. `pypdf`'s `extraction_mode="layout"` preserves x-position and showed the table has **three** columns (`IRMX` | `ICMX, I-1` | `I-2/3, I-P`) — the naive pass had merged stacked headers. Industrial districts are therefore **included**, not deferred. Had the flat text been seeded, IRMX and ICMX would have carried confident, wrong numbers.

## 6. Operational facts

- **SSH to the VM works** (§1). Write job scripts to `/tmp`, `docker compose cp` into `worker`, run with `docker compose exec -T worker python /tmp/<script>.py`. Clean up after.
- **Never run a job while a deploy is in flight** — `compose up -d` recreates `worker` and kills it.
- **The seed never deletes.** It upserts on `(market_code, code, code_version)`. A stale reference row survives forever and needs a migration.
- **Hand-write every Alembic migration.** Autogenerate is not safe here.
- **Parking ships with the code, not the seed** — it is a module constant, not reference data.
- Posture, buildability confidence, the program statement, valuation band and parking are **all computed on read**. No recompute, no version bump when they change.
- **Do not start `make dev` as a harness background task** — both servers get SIGTERM'd at the turn boundary. Use `nohup … & disown` from a foreground call, which survives.
- After a Docker restart, `mailpit` can fail `make up` with `network … not found`; `docker rm -f lotscope-mailpit-1` then retry.
- Philadelphia zoning codes have **no hyphen** in `parcel` (`RSA5`) but **are hyphenated** in `zoning_standard` (`RSA-5`).
- The dossier route is `/parcel/<market-slug>/<parcel-number>`.
