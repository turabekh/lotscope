## Incident & lessons (tile 404 outage — resolved)

Chasing the marker bug, I caused a transient prod tile outage. Two real problems surfaced, both fixed and
recorded in **[[map-tiles-serving]]**:

1. **Standalone Caddy `header` directive → intermittent 404s.** A `header Cache-Control` directive next to
   `reverse_proxy` (+ `encode gzip`) caused *flapping* 200/404 on `/tiles` (a directive/streaming race).
   Fixed by moving it inside `reverse_proxy` as `header_down` (`d89b417`).
2. **Repeated Martin restarts left Caddy's upstream pool stale.** Caddy resolves `martin` DNS + pools
   connections at start; restarting Martin changes its container IP, and a plain `caddy restart` didn't
   fully clear the pool → 404s. Fix: `docker compose up -d --force-recreate caddy` once Martin is stable.
- Also learned: **Martin returns 404 for HEAD requests** on tiles (only GET) — don't health-check with
  `curl -I`. And **Martin doesn't effectively cache** — the early "stale Martin cache" theory was a
  red herring that briefly broke prod; don't chase it.
