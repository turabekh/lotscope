### 2.1 Screening (ADR-0072) — and a recompute that mattered

One parcel showed both faults at once: *"Strong candidate — projected margin of 55% and no elevated risk signal"*, directly above three risk flags and "No comps".

The margin rested on a stand-in. With no AVM estimate the deal model falls back to the OPA assessed value, and `CRITICAL_FIELDS` covers only buildability, so valuation availability never reached the decision. And "no elevated risk signal" was true against a threshold of 60 at a score of 30 — and predictably read as "no risk".

Recompute of 14,474 rows, 27 minutes:

| | before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| `strong_candidate` | 14,474 | **10,053** |
| `worth_investigating` | 5,870 | **10,284** |

**4,421 parcels had been presenting as strong candidates on a margin priced from an assessment.**

### 2.2 The wide-area comp fallback (ADR-0073)

ADR-0072 was right and left those parcels saying nothing useful about price. Measured on a random sample of **400 of the actually-downgraded parcels**: 398 have zero comps; 220 recovered by radius 1,500→3,000 ft; 259 by radius and lookback; **338 by adding residential↔commercial interchange**.

Two measurements decided the design, and both are worth keeping:

- **Allowing improved-land sales recovers all 398 and is invalid** — such a sale prices the structure. It would produce a confident-looking number measuring the wrong thing.
- **Restricting cross-use to residential↔commercial costs nothing** — permitting all four classes also recovers exactly 338. Industrial and special contribute zero, so they stay segregated rather than being allowed for free.

The widened search runs **only when the tight query returns nothing**. Widening globally would pull distant sales into the 94% of parcels that already have good local comps, since comps are taken nearest-first up to a limit. A fallback cannot regress them by construction.

A wide-area estimate is forced to **`low` confidence** — the confidence formula reads only comp count and dispersion, and distance is not in it. It never earns `strong_candidate`. Mid-recompute, `strong_candidate` is holding at exactly 10,053, which is the intended invariant showing up in production.

**Fill in when the recompute lands:** final split, and how many of the 101,868 gained a value (~85% expected).

### 2.3 The near-miss worth remembering

The fallback is gated to markets where its window was measured — Philadelphia only, via a registry in `valuation/fallback.py`.

That gate exists because **`recompute_vacant_verdicts` runs nightly at 07:00 UTC with no market filter**. An ungated change would have reached every market the night it deployed, with nobody deciding it should. The pilots are far more exposed in proportion — 18.5% of Philadelphia verdicts carry no valuation, against **91.9% for Pittsburgh and 100% for Harrisburg** — and 3,000 ft spans a large share of a city the size of Harrisburg, where "wide-area" could amount to a citywide average wearing a local label.

Extending it to another market needs the same 400-parcel measurement first. Registered, not inherited — the discipline ADR-0057 set for distress profiles.

## 3. Process — what cost time, and what caught things

**`make check` could not catch a formatting failure CI enforces.** It ran `ruff check` and `pnpm lint` but neither format check. **`make fmt-check` now exists and `check` depends on it** — and it caught a real prettier failure within the hour.

**A green e2e suite proved nothing about the Risk tab**, because no spec ever opened it and every seeded parcel has a unique owner, so all six would have shown the blank. Two specs now open it.

**An assertion that depends on fixture data being unique is not owned by the fixture.** My first owner assertion broke on a machine that also has the demo dataset loaded (`Jane Smith` collides, `total_count` becomes 2). ADR-0071 documents this trap; it recurred anyway.

**I estimated the recompute wrong twice** — once low from batch count, once high from a partial sample. Local throughput overestimated production by ~2.6×. Measure the rate on the machine that will run it.
